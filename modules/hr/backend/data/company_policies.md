# Company Policies — Water Works Engineering

## Working Hours & Shifts

Standard working hours are 8 hours per day, 6 days a week. The company runs
four shifts: General (09:00–17:00), Morning (06:00–14:00), Evening
(14:00–22:00), and Night (22:00–06:00). Overtime beyond standard hours is paid
at 2x the hourly rate, calculated from your monthly salary. Shift changes must
be approved by your supervisor at least one day in advance.

## Attendance & Check-in

Attendance is recorded through the photo check-in link on your phone — open
the attendance link, allow camera and location access, and take a selfie. The
system recognises your face and marks you present; no badge or key is needed.
If your face is not recognised, contact HR to be enrolled. Check-ins outside
the site geofence are flagged for HR review. Repeated missed check-ins are
treated as absences.

## Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to 12 days of Sick Leave (SL), 12 days of Casual Leave
(CL), and 15 days of Annual Leave (AL) per calendar year. Leave requests are
submitted through the HR portal and require approval from HR or your manager.
Sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days requires a medical certificate.
Unused Annual Leave does not carry forward to the next year. Leave taken
beyond your remaining balance is unpaid.

## Payroll & Salary

Salaries are processed monthly and credited by the 7th of the following month.
Your salary is split into Basic, DA, HRA, conveyance, and medical allowance
components. PF (12% of Basic+DA) and ESI (0.75% of gross, if applicable) are
deducted as per statutory rules. Payslips are available from HR. Salary
advances are recovered in installments as agreed at the time of the advance.

## Expense Reimbursement

Business expenses (travel, food, accommodation, office supplies) can be
claimed through the expenses portal with a photo of the receipt. Claims must
be submitted within 30 days of the expense. Approved claims are paid out with
the next monthly payroll as a non-taxable reimbursement. Claims above ₹5,000
need Finance approval with original receipts.

## IT & Asset Policy

Company equipment (laptops, phones, tools) is assigned through the IT asset
register and remains company property. Report loss or damage to IT within 24
hours. All assigned assets must be returned on your last working day; pending
assets delay the full & final settlement. Personal use of company internet is
permitted within reason; accessing prohibited content is a disciplinary
offence.

## Code of Conduct

Treat colleagues with respect; harassment or discrimination of any kind is
grounds for immediate disciplinary action. Safety gear must be worn at all
work sites. Alcohol and intoxicants are strictly prohibited during work hours.
Company information, drawings, and client data are confidential and must not
be shared outside the company.

## Exit & Resignation

Resignations require 30 days written notice to HR. During the notice period,
complete the offboarding checklist: return assets, hand over work, and clear
dues. The full & final settlement, including unpaid salary and leave
encashment where applicable, is processed within 30 days of the last working
day.
